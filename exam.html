PAPER STRUCTURE

How the full-length paper works

MCQ answers are selectable on screen; subjective questions are displayed for solving on paper.

01 · MCQ Section

First 15 questions. Each MCQ will show answer options that can be selected directly in the exam interface. Answer selection is saved for the attempt.

02 · Subjective Section

After the 15 MCQs. Subjective questions are displayed with their marks/structure for the student to solve on paper. They are not treated as on-screen typed answers.

Timer: The selected exam duration is configured before starting. Default is 3 Hours 15 Minutes. The countdown starts only when the exam begins.
